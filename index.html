How it works

  1. Set up. Type your name, pick a hero and a sidekick.
  2. Walk the road. Each village is five questions.
  3. Beat the clock. Quick answers earn bonus aura.
  4. Win badges. Score 80% to clear the village and unlock the next chapter of the story.

For parents

Short, low-stakes practice built around Grade 4 / Primary 4 Maths outcomes.

  • What it covers: times tables, place value, add and subtract, fractions, money, time, division, rounding, area and perimeter, word problems, factors, Roman numerals, multiplication, mixed numbers, decimals, units, shapes and graphs. English is on the way.
  • How mastery is judged: an outcome counts as secure after 80% or more on two different days, so a lucky run is not enough.
  • No pressure: no lives and no game over. A wrong answer gets a retry and a friendly nudge.
  • Sound: chiptune music and effects, with switches for each in the game.
  • Private: no accounts, no ads, no chat. Progress stays on the device.
